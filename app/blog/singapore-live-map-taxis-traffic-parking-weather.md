# Singapore Live Map: Taxis, Traffic, Parking and Rain in One Free App

Before you leave home in Singapore you might check four apps: one to see whether taxis are around, one for traffic, one for a parking lot and one to see whether it is about to pour. **Singapore MRT Bus Card Balance** now puts all of that on one map, in a new **Live** view.

The app already read your EZ-Link and NETS FlashPay balance and showed the MRT and bus network. Version 1.6.0 adds a third map view next to *Metro* and *Bus*. You switch on only the layers you want, and each one is drawn straight from Singapore's official open data.

## What You Can Switch On

The Live view has 12 layers in three groups.

**Roads and taxis**

- **Taxis.** Every available taxi LTA reports, refreshed every 30 seconds. On a normal afternoon that is thousands of dots across the island.
- **Taxi stands.** Every official stand, with its name, its code and whether it is barrier-free.
- **Traffic incidents.** Accidents, roadworks, breakdowns and heavy traffic, with LTA's own message for each one.
- **Traffic cameras.** Tap a camera to see its latest photo of the road.
- **Road signs.** The messages on the electronic signs above the expressways.
- **Flood alerts.** PUB flood warnings, when there are any.

**Parking and charging**

- **Carparks.** Free lots right now, for cars, motorcycles and heavy vehicles, in HDB, URA and LTA carparks.
- **EV charging.** Charging points with how many are free, occupied or out of service, and which plugs they take.
- **Bicycle parking.** Racks near the part of the map you are looking at, with the rack count and whether they are sheltered.

**Weather**

- **2-hour forecast.** The forecast for each area of Singapore.
- **Rainfall.** The latest reading at each rain gauge.
- **Temperature.** The latest air temperature at each weather station.

## Why One Map Helps

Each layer answers a question you would otherwise open a separate app for:

| You want to know | Switch on |
|---|---|
| Can I get a taxi here, or should I walk to the stand? | Taxis, Taxi stands |
| Is the CTE jammed right now? | Traffic cameras, Traffic incidents |
| Will there be a lot when I arrive at the mall? | Carparks |
| Where can I charge my EV on the way? | EV charging |
| Should I take an umbrella? | 2-hour forecast, Rainfall |

Because they share one map, you can mix them. Switch on rainfall and taxis together on a wet evening, or carparks and EV charging before a weekend drive.

## Honest About Where the Data Comes From

Everything in the Live view comes from **LTA DataMall** (taxis, traffic, parking, EV charging, bicycle parking and PUB flood alerts) and **data.gov.sg** (the weather). The app shows each source's own words and numbers. It never invents an estimate.

- Each layer refreshes no faster than its source updates: taxis every 30 seconds, carparks and incidents every minute, weather every 5 minutes.
- If a layer cannot refresh for a while, its old dots are removed rather than passed off as current.
- A layer only loads while it is switched on and the Live view is on screen, so it does not use data in the background.

Live data can still be late or missing, so check the official source for anything that really matters.

## Still a Card Reader First

The Live view is an addition. The app still does what it was built for:

- **Tap your EZ-Link or NETS FlashPay card** to see the balance in Singapore dollars and your home currency.
- **Keep every trip** the card has ever shown, not just its last 30.
- **Find your way** with the MRT/LRT street map, live bus arrivals and station crowd levels.

There is no account and no sign-up. Your card data stays on your phone.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is the Live map free?**
Yes. The app is free with ads, and a one-time purchase removes the ads.

**Do I need an account?**
No. Nothing to sign up for.

**Does it track my location?**
No. Location is only used, if you allow it, to centre the map on you while the app is open. One layer, Bicycle parking, sends the centre of the map you are looking at, rounded to about 110 metres, to LTA to find racks nearby.

**Is it an official LTA app?**
No. It is an independent app that uses LTA's and data.gov.sg's open data. It is not affiliated with LTA, PUB, NEA, EZ-Link or NETS.
