# How to Block YouTube Shorts on Android, Without Losing YouTube

You open YouTube for a tutorial, a lecture or a podcast. Then a Short starts playing, and another, and another. YouTube lets you hide Shorts for a while, but they come back, and there's no permanent off switch.

Here's how to block **only YouTube Shorts** on Android, so YouTube is still there for what you actually opened it for.

## Block Shorts with FeedLock

**FeedLock** closes the Shorts player the moment it opens, whether you tap the Shorts tab, a Short in your home feed, or one a friend sent you.

1. **Install FeedLock** and finish setup. It needs *FeedLock Protection* (an Android Accessibility setting) to recognise the Shorts player. It never records or sends what's on your screen.
2. **Go to the Apps tab, switch on YouTube and choose "Feed only" → Shorts.**
3. **On Home, pick a length and tap the round dial** to start a session.

While the session runs, Shorts close instantly and send you back. Everything else on YouTube works as usual.

| Subscriptions and playlists | Shorts from the home feed or shared links |

## Tips for Students and Remote Workers

- **Study sessions:** start a 2-hour session before you open lecture videos. Free strict sessions last up to 2 hours; Pro goes up to 24.
- **No more "just one Short" at night:** with Pro, a schedule can start a strict session every evening on its own.
- **Want YouTube gone completely while you work?** Choose **Whole app** instead of **Feed only**.

## Is It Private?

Yes. There's no account. FeedLock only looks at which app and screen is in front, on your phone, to close the feeds you picked. Your viewing history and anything you type never leave your phone.
