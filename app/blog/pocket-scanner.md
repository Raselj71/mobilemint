# Pocket Scanner: Your Document Toolkit in One App

**Pocket Scanner** turns your phone into a document scanner and a complete PDF editor. Point your camera to capture receipts, IDs, notes, or whiteboards — automatic page detection gives you crisp, clean scans, and built-in OCR makes every page searchable and copyable.

## All Your PDF Tools in One Place

With Pocket Scanner, you get a whole document toolkit in a single app:

- **Scan** documents, receipts, IDs, notes, and whiteboards with automatic page detection
- **Edit** PDFs: merge, split, reorder, rotate, and compress files
- **Sign & protect**: Add signatures, watermarks, password-protect files, and redact private information
- **Open any format**: Read PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, EPUB, and text files in a clean viewer with dark mode

> **Everything works fully offline** — your files never leave your device, and no account is required.

## Privacy You Can Trust

Pocket Scanner works entirely on your device. Your scans and documents never leave your phone. The app is free and supported by ads, but it does not share your data with third parties.

- **Camera**: Used only while scanning to capture pages. Images are processed on your device.
- **Documents & files**: The scans and files you open are stored locally on your device. We never upload or see them.
- **Advertising ID**: Used by our ad partner to display ads. It is not linked to your identity or your documents.
