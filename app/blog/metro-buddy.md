# Metro Buddy: Your Dhaka MRT Balance, Instantly

**Metro Buddy** lets you check your Dhaka Metro Rail MRT Pass or Rapid Pass balance and trip history in seconds — just tap the card to the back of your phone. No internet, no account, no waiting at the station machine.

## Offline & Read-Only

Check your balance and journeys without ever connecting to the internet:

- **Instant balance** — Tap your MRT or Rapid Pass to the back of your phone and your balance appears at once
- **Trips & spending** — Recent journeys with station in/out and fares, plus monthly spend and travel stats
- **Fares & route map** — An interactive MRT Line 6 map showing the fare between any two stations
- **Private & offline** — Works 100% offline and read-only — no account, no ads, and nothing leaves your phone

> **Bangla-first, with an English toggle and a beautiful dark mode.**

## Privacy & Data

Metro Buddy is 100% offline and read-only. It reads your MRT / Rapid Pass card on your device only — there is no account, no ads, no analytics, and nothing ever leaves your phone.

- **NFC card reading** — Used only to read your transit card's balance and recent trips when you tap it. The app can read the card but never modify it.
- **Card data (on device)** — Balances, trips, and nicknames are stored locally on your device so you can view them later. They are never uploaded.
