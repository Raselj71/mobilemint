# AI Voice Recorder: Record Anything, Stay Private

**AI Voice Recorder** is built for the recordings you cannot afford to lose. It captures through a foreground service, so the recording keeps running when you switch apps or lock the screen, and it handles interruptions properly — an incoming call pauses the capture and resumes it afterwards instead of silently ending it.

## Record Without Compromise

Everything happens on your phone. No servers. No accounts. No compromises:

- **Foreground service recording** — keeps running even when you switch apps or lock your screen
- **Incoming call handling** — pauses capture and resumes afterwards, never loses the take
- **On-device transcription** — turn a recording into searchable text on the phone. The audio is never uploaded to transcribe it.
- **Markers** — drop a bookmark mid-recording and jump straight back to that moment later
- **Pro features** — remove every ad, unlock transcription, markers, capture settings, and batch export

> **Your recordings are written straight to your device's storage.** Transcription runs locally. No account is ever required.

## Privacy & Data

AI Voice Recorder records and stores your audio entirely on your device — we have no server, and your recordings are never uploaded to us or to anyone else. Transcription also runs on the phone. No account is required. The app is free and supported by ads, and Pro removes every ad and unlocks transcription, markers, capture settings, and batch export.

- **Microphone**: Used only while you are recording, to capture audio. The audio is written directly to your device and is never transmitted to us.
- **Audio recordings & files**: Your recordings are stored locally on your device. They leave the phone only when you yourself share or export one.
- **Advertising ID**: Used by our ad partner to display ads and limit how often the same ad repeats. It is not linked to your identity and never to the contents of your recordings. Pro removes ads.
- **Purchase status**: Google Play tells the app whether you have an active Pro subscription so it can unlock Pro features. We never see or store your payment details.
- **Notifications**: Used to show the recording controls and elapsed time while a recording is running in the background, and to deliver any notification you opt into.
- **Crash logs & performance**: If the app crashes, where in the code it happened plus your device model and Android version. Never a recording, a title or a transcript.
- **Anonymous app events**: Counts such as "a recording was started". The list of events is fixed in the source, and event data is limited to numbers and true/false flags, so no title or transcript can be included.
