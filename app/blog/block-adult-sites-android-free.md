# How to Block Adult Sites on Android for Free, Without a VPN

Want to keep porn and gambling sites off your phone, or a family member's? You don't need a paid app or a VPN that slows everything down. Android has a built-in setting called **Private DNS** that can do it, in every app and every browser.

## How Private DNS Blocking Works

Every time an app opens a website, your phone first asks a *DNS server* where that site lives. **Private DNS** lets you choose which server answers, over an encrypted connection.

Point it at a **family filter** and the server simply refuses to look up adult and gambling sites, so they can't load. Normal sites work as usual.

- ✅ Works in **every app and browser** at once
- ✅ **Free**, with no VPN and no key icon in your status bar
- ✅ Built into Android 9 and newer

You can set this up without any app. Go to **Settings → (Network / Connections) → Private DNS → Private DNS provider hostname**, and enter `family.adguard-dns.com`. FeedLock just makes it easier and tells you when it's on.
