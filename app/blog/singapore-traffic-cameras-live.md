# Singapore Traffic Cameras Live: Check Jams, Accidents and Expressway Signs

Is the PIE crawling? Has there been an accident on the CTE? Is the Causeway backed up? A traffic camera photo answers that faster than any estimate. **Singapore MRT Bus Card Balance** now shows LTA's traffic cameras, incidents and expressway signs together, in its new **Live** map view.

## Traffic Cameras: See the Road Yourself

Switch on **Traffic cameras** and each camera LTA publishes appears on the map. Tap one to see its **latest photo** of the road.

- The photo is LTA's own image, so you see the actual traffic, not a coloured line someone calculated.
- The layer refreshes every **minute**.
- Camera links from LTA expire after a few minutes, so the app shows the photo and never keeps it.

How many cameras appear depends on what LTA is publishing at the time.

## Traffic Incidents: What Is Happening, in LTA's Words

Switch on **Traffic incidents** to see LTA's current list of:

- **accidents**
- **vehicle breakdowns**
- **roadworks**
- **heavy traffic** and other obstacles

Each one is an orange dot. Tap it to read LTA's own message, for example which lane is affected and in which direction. The layer refreshes every **minute**.

## Road Signs: What the Expressway Signs Say

The electronic signs above Singapore's expressways warn of jams and accidents ahead. Switch on **Road signs** to read every sign's current message on your phone, before you reach it. This layer refreshes every **2 minutes**.

## Plan a Drive in a Minute

1. Open the **Map** tab and tap **Live**.
2. Switch on **Traffic incidents**, **Traffic cameras** and **Road signs**.
3. Look along your route for orange incident dots, then tap the nearest camera to see the actual traffic.

Add **Rainfall** on a wet day: heavy rain plus an incident usually means a slow drive.

## Who It Helps

- **Drivers** choosing between two expressways.
- **Commuters to Johor Bahru** checking the Woodlands and Tuas side before leaving, using the cameras LTA publishes near the checkpoints.
- **Anyone catching a taxi or bus** who wants to know whether the roads are clear.

## Data and Privacy

All three layers come from **LTA DataMall** (Traffic Images, Traffic Incidents and VMS). The app asks LTA directly, with no server of ours in between, and only while a layer is switched on and the Live view is on screen. Nothing about you is sent beyond what any web request carries.

Live data can be late or missing. For anything urgent, check the official LTA channels.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Are the camera photos live video?**
No. They are the latest still images LTA publishes, refreshed every minute.

**Does the app give travel times or route around jams?**
No. It shows LTA's cameras, incidents and sign messages as they are. It does not calculate travel times or reroute you.

**Is it free?**
Yes, free with ads. A one-time purchase removes the ads.

**Is this an official LTA app?**
No. It is an independent app using LTA's open data, not affiliated with LTA.
