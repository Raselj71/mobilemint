# Will It Rain in Singapore Today? Rainfall, 2-Hour Forecast and Flood Alerts on One Map

In Singapore the sky can go from sunny to a downpour in twenty minutes, and it can pour in Jurong while Tampines stays dry. A single forecast for "Singapore" is not much help. You want to know about **your** area, **right now**.

**Singapore MRT Bus Card Balance** now has a **Live** map view with three weather layers from **data.gov.sg** and PUB flood alerts from LTA, all on the same map.

## 2-Hour Forecast for Every Area

Switch on **2-hour forecast** and every forecast area in Singapore shows its forecast as a label on the map: *Fair*, *Cloudy*, *Light Showers*, *Thundery Showers* and so on. Tap an area to see the period the forecast covers.

This is the official 2-hour nowcast, refreshed every **5 minutes**. Because it is split by area, you can see whether rain is expected where you are, where you are going, or both.

## Rainfall: Where Is It Raining Now?

The forecast tells you what might happen. **Rainfall** tells you what is happening. Switch it on and each rain gauge across the island shows its latest reading in millimetres. Gauges recording rain are highlighted, so a band of showers moving across the island is easy to spot.

## Temperature

Switch on **Temperature** for the latest air temperature at each weather station. That is handy for planning a run or deciding whether a walk to the MRT is pleasant or punishing.

## Flood Alerts from PUB

Heavy rain in Singapore can flood low-lying roads quickly. Switch on **Flood alerts** under *Roads and taxis* to see PUB's current flood warnings, passed on through LTA. Tap an alert for:

- the **area** affected
- PUB's **advice**
- its **severity**
- when it was **issued** and when it **expires**

When there are no alerts, the switch says so plainly: *No active flood alerts*.

## How to Use It

1. Open the **Map** tab and tap **Live**.
2. Under **Weather**, switch on **2-hour forecast**, **Rainfall** or **Temperature**. Under **Roads and taxis**, switch on **Flood alerts**.
3. Close the panel, zoom to your area and tap any label or dot for details.

## Combine It with the Rest of the Map

- **Rain and taxis.** When it starts pouring, see where the taxis are.
- **Rain and traffic cameras.** Check whether the downpour has slowed the expressway.
- **Rain and the MRT.** Switch back to the *Metro* view to see how crowded your station is and where its exits are.

## Data and Privacy

- Forecast, rainfall and temperature come from **data.gov.sg**, the Singapore Government's open data portal. Flood alerts come from **PUB** through **LTA DataMall**.
- The app asks them directly, with no server of ours in between, and only while a layer is on and the Live view is on screen.
- Nothing about you is sent beyond what any web request carries.

Weather data can be late. For safety decisions during severe weather, follow PUB's and NEA's official channels.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is this a full weather app?**
No. It shows the official 2-hour forecast, rain gauge readings, temperatures and flood alerts on the map. There are no long-range forecasts.

**How often does it update?**
The weather layers refresh every 5 minutes, flood alerts every 3 minutes.

**Is it free?**
Yes, free with ads. A one-time purchase removes the ads.

**Is this an official government app?**
No. It is an independent app using data.gov.sg's and LTA's open data, not affiliated with NEA, PUB or LTA.
