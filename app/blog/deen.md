# Deen: Your Offline Worship Companion

**Deen** is a calm, ad-light companion for daily worship, designed in Bangla from the ground up. Get accurate prayer times for your location, gentle adhan reminders, and a qibla compass that works offline.

## Read the Quran, Anytime, Anywhere

Deen offers a seamless Quran reading experience:

- **Holy Quran with translation** — read and bookmark your favorite verses, fully offline
- **Adhan reminders** — precise prayer-time notifications with a choice of adhans
- **Qibla finder** — point toward the Kaaba with an accurate on-device compass
- **Bangla-first** — crafted in Bangla, with more languages built in

> **Your data stays on your device.** Deen asks for the minimum it needs and keeps your data private.

## Privacy & Data

Deen works offline and needs no account. It uses your approximate location only to calculate prayer times and qibla direction on your device — you can also set your city manually. The app is free and ad-supported.

- **Approximate location**: Used on-device to compute prayer times and qibla. Optional — you can set your city manually.
- **Notifications**: To deliver adhan and prayer-time reminders you enable.
- **Advertising ID**: Used by our ad partner to show ads and limit repetition.
- **Anonymous app diagnostics**: Crash logs and aggregated usage to improve the app.
