# How to Block Instagram Reels on Android, and Keep Your DMs

You open Instagram to answer one message. Forty minutes later you're still swiping Reels. Instagram has no switch to turn Reels off, and deleting the app means losing your DMs, stories and friends too.

This guide shows how to block **just the Reels and Explore feeds** on Android, so Instagram stays useful and stops being a time sink.

## Why Not Just Delete Instagram?

- You still need **messages**. Many friends, groups and businesses only reply on Instagram.
- Deleting is **all or nothing**. Most people reinstall within days.
- **Reels are designed to be endless.** Willpower against a feed tuned to keep you watching is a losing game. Removing the feed works better.

## Block Reels with FeedLock in 3 Steps

**FeedLock** is a free Android app that closes the Reels and Explore feeds the moment they open, and leaves the rest of Instagram alone.

1. **Install FeedLock and finish the short setup.** It asks you to turn on *FeedLock Protection* (Android's Accessibility setting). That's how it recognises a Reel on your screen. Nothing you see is recorded or sent.
2. **Open the Apps tab, switch on Instagram and choose "Feed only".** Pick **Reels**, and **Explore** too if that's where you lose time. On the free plan, feed-only blocking covers one app.
3. **On Home, tap the round dial to start a session.** Choose a length first, such as 1 hour or *Until end*.

That's it. Tap a Reel and you're sent straight back out. Your DMs, stories, profile and search keep working.

## Make It Stick

- **Use Strict mode** for exam weeks or deep work. A strict session can't be ended early on a whim, and FeedLock can't be uninstalled while it runs. A free 30-minute cooldown is there if you really need out.
- **Check the Stats tab.** Seeing "blocked 14 times today" is a surprisingly strong nudge.
- **With Pro, schedules** start a session on their own, for example weekdays 9:00–17:00 or every night before bed.

## FAQ

**Does FeedLock read my messages?**
No. It only checks which app and which feed is on screen so it can close the feeds you chose. What you see and type is never recorded, stored or sent.

**Does it work on iPhone?**
No. FeedLock is Android only.

**Can I block the whole Instagram app instead?**
Yes. Choose **Whole app** instead of **Feed only**. The free plan covers two whole apps.
