# GoLive Studio: Multistream to YouTube and Facebook at Once

**GoLive Studio** turns your phone into a multistreaming studio. Point your camera or share your screen and broadcast to YouTube and Facebook Live simultaneously — no laptop, capture card, or encoder required.

## Go Live in Seconds

Broadcast your camera or screen to multiple platforms at the same time:

- **Multistream at once** — One tap sends your broadcast to YouTube and Facebook together
- **Camera or screen** — Go live from your camera or share whatever's on your screen
- **Your own accounts** — Connect your channels and pages securely — you keep full control
- **No computer needed** — A complete streaming setup that lives entirely on your phone

> **Sign in with your own YouTube and Facebook accounts.** GoLive Studio only sends the stream you start, when you start it. You stay in control of your channels and pages.

## Privacy & Data

GoLive Studio streams to the YouTube and Facebook accounts you connect. We use those platforms' official sign-in to get permission to start streams on your behalf — we never see or store your passwords, and we only broadcast the streams you start. Your video is sent directly to the platforms you choose, not to us.

- **Google / YouTube account** — Used via Google sign-in (OAuth) only to create and start live streams on your YouTube channel. We never receive your password.
- **Facebook account** — Used via Facebook Login only to publish live videos to the pages or profile you choose. We never receive your password.
- **Camera & microphone** — Captured only while you are live to send your audio and video to the platforms you selected. The stream is not stored by us.
- **Screen recording** — Captured only when you choose to share your screen, and only while you are live.
- **Advertising ID** — Used by our ad partner to show ads and limit repetition.
- **Anonymous app diagnostics** — Crash logs and aggregated usage to improve the app.
