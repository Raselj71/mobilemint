# All Document Reader: One App for Every Format

**All Document Reader** opens virtually any file you throw at it — PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, and plain text — in a single, fast app. So you don't need a separate viewer for every format.

## Open Anything, Anytime

From quick glances to deep reviews, All Document Reader handles it all:

- **PDF files** — read, scroll, and navigate with ease
- **Word documents** (.doc, .docx) — preserve formatting and layout
- **Excel spreadsheets** (.xls, .xlsx) — view data and formulas
- **PowerPoint presentations** (.ppt, .pptx) — review slides and notes
- **Plain text** (.txt) — simple, clean reading experience

> **A smart library organises your files by type and recents.** Large documents open instantly, and everything is read locally on your device. It's free, lightweight, and gets out of your way.

## Privacy & Data

All Document Reader opens the files you choose directly on your device — we never upload them, and the app collects no personal data. It is free, with ads and optional in-app purchases, and does not share your data with third parties.

- **Files & storage access**: Used only to open and display the documents you choose. Files are read on your device and never uploaded.
- **Advertising ID**: Used by our ad partner to display ads. It is not linked to your identity or your files.
