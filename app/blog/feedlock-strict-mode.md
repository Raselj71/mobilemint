# Strict Mode: Why Most Focus Apps Fail, and How FeedLock's Holds

Every blocker works on day one. The trouble is day four, at 11 pm, when the urge to scroll wins and the "turn off" button is one tap away. If quitting is easy, you'll quit.

**FeedLock's strict mode** is built for exactly that moment.

## What Makes a Session "Strict"

Turn on **Strict mode** on Home, choose a length, and tap the round dial. Until the timer runs out:

- 🔒 **No early exit.** The dial shows the time left. Tapping it opens unlock options, not an off switch.
- 🧱 **Blocks can't be weakened.** You can add more apps to block, but removing one waits until the session ends.
- 🛡️ **Uninstall protection.** FeedLock can't be removed while a strict session is running.
- ⚙️ **Settings shield.** FeedLock guards the Settings pages that could switch it off during the session.

## When You Genuinely Need Out

Life happens. Strict mode gives you two honest ways out:

| Option | How it works |
|--------|--------------|
| ⏳ **Free cooldown** | Stay on a 30-minute timer. Leave the screen and it starts over, which is long enough for the urge to pass. |
| ⚡ **Instant unlock** | End the session right away with a small one-time payment. It never renews. |

The cooldown is the point: most urges to scroll fade in a few minutes, and having to wait out 30 of them makes the choice deliberate.

## Never Silently Unprotected

Android can switch an app's accessibility service off, after an update, a battery saver, or someone fiddling with Settings. When that happens, blocking stops. FeedLock makes sure you know:

- A **"Blocking is off"** notification appears the moment FeedLock Protection is switched off, with one tap to turn it back on.
- A running session's notification changes to **"Blocking paused"**.
- If it stays off, you're reminded again later. It doesn't nag every few minutes.

## How Long Can a Session Last?

- **Free:** strict sessions up to **2 hours**.
- **Pro:** up to **24 hours**, plus **schedules** (for example weekdays 9:00–17:00) and a **daily limit** that starts a strict session when your time is up.

## Start Your First Strict Session

1. Choose the apps or feeds to block on the **Apps** tab.
2. On **Home**, switch on **Strict mode** and pick a length.
3. Tap the dial. Your phone is now on your side.
