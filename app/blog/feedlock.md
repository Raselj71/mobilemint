# FeedLock: Kill the Feed, Keep the App

Most screen-time apps give you two choices: keep Instagram and lose an evening to Reels, or delete it and lose your messages too. **FeedLock** gives you a third: it closes the endless feed and leaves the rest of the app alone.

Open a Reel and you're sent back out. Open your DMs, search for a friend or post a story, and everything works as normal.

## What FeedLock Blocks

| App | Feed FeedLock can close | What keeps working |
|-----|------------------------|--------------------|
| 📸 **Instagram** | Reels, Explore | Messages, your feed, stories, search, posting |
| ▶️ **YouTube** | Shorts | Normal videos, subscriptions, search |
| 👍 **Facebook** | Reels | Messages, groups, your feed |
| 👻 **Snapchat** | Spotlight | Chats, stories, the camera |

Want an app gone completely during a session? Switch it to **Whole app** and FeedLock closes it as soon as it opens.

## How It Works

1. **Pick what to block.** On the **Apps** tab, switch on an app and choose **Feed only** or **Whole app**.
2. **Start a session.** On **Home**, choose a length (Until end, 1 hour, 2 hours, 4 hours or Custom) and tap the round dial.
3. **Get on with your day.** While the session runs, blocked feeds close the moment they open. Tap the dial again when you're done.

## A Strict Mode That Actually Holds

Turn on **Strict mode** and the session can't be ended on a whim. Changes that weaken your blocks wait until the timer runs out, and uninstall protection keeps FeedLock in place. If you really need out, a free 30-minute cooldown is always there.

## Privacy

There's no account. FeedLock uses Android's Accessibility service only on your phone, to recognise the feeds you chose to block and close them. What you see, what you type and which apps you use are never recorded or sent. Your rules, sessions and stats stay on your device.
