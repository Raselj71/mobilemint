# Singapore Carpark Availability Live: Free Lots, EV Chargers and Bicycle Parking

Circling a full carpark is a waste of time and petrol. Driving to a charger only to find every point taken is worse. **Singapore MRT Bus Card Balance** now has a **Live** map view that shows free lots, free chargers and bicycle racks before you set off.

## Carparks: Free Lots Right Now

Switch on the **Carparks** layer and every carpark LTA reports appears, with its number of free car lots beside it. A carpark with none free is shown in red.

Tap a carpark to see:

- its **name** (the development or area)
- the **agency** that runs it: HDB, URA or LTA
- **free lots by type**: cars, motorcycles and heavy vehicles, when the carpark has them

The layer refreshes every **minute**, the same as LTA's feed. It covers the carparks in **LTA DataMall's Carpark Availability** data, which includes HDB carparks, URA carparks and the major malls and developments LTA lists. Not every private carpark in Singapore is in that feed, so a building that is missing from the map is simply not reported.

## EV Charging: Which Points Are Free

Switch on **EV charging** to see charging locations across the island. Each dot shows how many points are free out of the total, for example **4/5**. Tap one for:

- the **address**
- how many points are **available**, **occupied** and **not available** (out of service)
- the **plug types** at that location

Green means at least one point is free. Amber means they are all in use. Red means none are working. The layer refreshes every **5 minutes**, from LTA's EV charging data.

## Bicycle Parking Near Where You Are Looking

Cycling to the MRT? Switch on **Bicycle parking** and the racks near the part of the map in view appear, each with:

- the **number of racks**
- the **rack type**
- whether it is **sheltered**

Bicycle parking is the one layer that needs to know where you are looking. It sends the centre of the map, rounded to about 110 metres, to LTA to find nearby racks. It is never sent to us. Pan the map, and the racks for the new area load.

## How to Use It

1. Open the **Map** tab and tap **Live**.
2. Under **Parking and charging**, tap **Carparks**, **EV charging** or **Bicycle parking**.
3. Each switch shows how many places are on the map. Close the panel and tap any dot for its details.

## Good Times to Check

- **Weekend malls.** Check the carpark at your destination before you leave.
- **HDB visits.** See whether the block's carpark has lots before you drive over.
- **EV road trips.** Find a free charger on your route, and see the plug type before you arrive.
- **Cycling to the MRT.** Find a sheltered rack near the station.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is the carpark data live?**
It refreshes every minute from LTA DataMall. A lot can still be taken between the update and your arrival.

**Does it include season parking or prices?**
No. It shows free lots only. LTA's feed has no rates, so the app shows none.

**Is it free?**
Yes, free with ads. A one-time purchase removes the ads.

**Is this an official app?**
No. It is an independent app using LTA's open data, not affiliated with LTA, HDB or URA.
