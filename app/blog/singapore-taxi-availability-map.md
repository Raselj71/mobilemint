# Can't Find a Taxi in Singapore? See Every Available Taxi on a Live Map

It is 11 pm, it has just started raining, and the ride-hailing app keeps saying "looking for a driver". Are there any taxis around, or are they all on the other side of the island?

**Singapore MRT Bus Card Balance** can now answer that. Its new **Live** map view shows every available taxi LTA reports, as a dot on the map, refreshed every 30 seconds.

## What the Taxi Layer Shows

- **Available taxis.** LTA's Taxi Availability feed lists the taxis that are free to pick up a passenger. The app draws each one as a small amber dot. On a weekday afternoon that is usually thousands of taxis.
- **Refreshed every 30 seconds.** That matches how often LTA updates the feed, so the dots move as taxis drive and get hired.
- **Where the taxis are, at a glance.** Dense clusters mean a good chance of a cab, and an empty patch means you should walk somewhere busier.

The dots are positions only. LTA does not say which company a taxi belongs to or where it is heading, so the app does not either.

## Pair It with Taxi Stands

Switch on **Taxi stands** as well, and every official stand appears. Tap one to see:

- its **name** and **stand code**
- whether it is a **stand or a stop**, and who runs it
- whether it is **barrier-free**

If the taxis are bunched near a mall's stand a few hundred metres away, you know where to walk.

## How to Use It

1. Open the **Map** tab and tap **Live**.
2. In the Live layers panel, tap **Taxis**, and **Taxi stands** if you want them.
3. The panel shows how many taxis are on the map. Close it to see the dots, and pinch to zoom into your area.
4. Tap the location button to centre the map on you, if you allow location.

You can switch on other layers at the same time. On a wet night, **Rainfall** and **Taxis** together show where it is pouring and where the cabs are.

## Why Use This Instead of Just Waiting?

- **Know before you commit.** If there are no taxis near you, book early or head for the MRT.
- **Spot a better pickup point.** Busy roads and malls often have taxis when side streets have none.
- **Late-night planning.** See whether it is worth waiting for a taxi or taking a night bus.

## Data and Privacy

- Taxi positions come from **LTA DataMall's Taxi Availability** feed. Taxi stands come from LTA's **Taxi Stands** list.
- The app asks LTA directly, with no server of ours in between, and only while the taxi layer is on and the Live view is on screen.
- Your location is used only on your phone, to centre the map on you. It is never sent to us or to LTA by this layer.

Live data can be late. A taxi shown as available may already be taken by the time you reach it.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I book a taxi in the app?**
No. It shows where available taxis are. It does not book them.

**Why can't I tap a taxi?**
LTA's feed gives only positions, with no details to show, so taxis are plain dots.

**Is it free?**
Yes, free with ads. A one-time purchase removes the ads.

**Is this an official LTA app?**
No. It is an independent app that uses LTA's open data. It is not affiliated with LTA or any taxi company.
